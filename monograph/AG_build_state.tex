\markboth{Build record}{Build record}

The repository was built with \texttt{python build\_all.py -{}-full} on 24 September 2026 (Python
3.12.4, Windows~11). The build compiles every paper with \texttt{pdflatex}, compiles this volume
with \texttt{pdflatex}, \texttt{bibtex} and \texttt{pdflatex} twice, and reruns every verification
script with its full computation. A script passes when it exits normally and its final line reports all
of its checks passed.

\noindent All $17$ papers and this volume compile with no error and no undefined reference; this volume
has no overfull line, and three of the standalone papers have four slightly overfull lines between
them, which the build reports as warnings. Every verification script passes all of its checks.

\paragraph{The long computations.} All of them were rerun from scratch in this build, with no
checkpoint available to the scripts:
\begin{itemize}
\item the connected count of Chapter~\ref{chap:ch08} at $k=10$, $909\,103\,210$ points
($81$ minutes);
\item the enumeration of the $1\,240\,843\,950$ half-box points of Chapter~\ref{chap:ch09}, giving
$\#\DS(\phi\mathrm X174,10)=31\,925\,753\,246\,212$ ($39$ minutes);
\item the signed-quotient battery of Chapter~\ref{chap:ch06} ($14$ minutes);
\item the proteome computation of Chapter~\ref{chap:ch12} and its figures ($11$ minutes);
\item the eight-strain pan-genome of Chapter~\ref{chap:ch14}, $\RecGrp=\Z^{117\,848}$ with its
freeness certificate ($3$ minutes).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Comparison with the reference transcripts.} After timings are removed, $14$ of the $20$
transcripts with a reference are identical to it line for line. In the other six every check passes as
in the reference and every exact result is the same; the differences are these.
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{ds\_count.py}: the reference was saved in a different text encoding; decoded, it has the
same counts.
\item \texttt{real\_pangenome.py}: the transcript now begins with the header lines that the script
writes to its output file.
\item \texttt{repeat\_splitting.py}: two diagnostic dictionaries are printed with their entries in a
different order.
\item \texttt{ds\_fermion.py}: the reference was saved from a quick run; the full run adds the progress
lines of the abandoned frontier attempts described in Section~\ref{ch09:sec:wall}.
\item \texttt{bio14\_verify.py}: the time per vertex in Table~\ref{ch16:tab:scale} was $10.6$, $10.8$
and $11.9\,\mu$s in this build, against $9.2$, $9.3$ and $10.7\,\mu$s in the text. Timings depend on
the machine and its load; the fronts and the agreement modulo both primes are unchanged.
\item \texttt{spectral\_fibres.py}: the floating-point check of Section~\ref{ch12:sec:proteome} draws
its $200$ test sequences at random and found a worst relative error of $6.2\cdot10^{-16}$, against the
$6.5\cdot10^{-16}$ quoted. Two alignment ratios that the reference left undefined are now computed, and
they are the values $0.981$ and $0.986$ of Table~\ref{ch12:tab:mates}.
\end{itemize}
